Questions and answers

Do I need to prove that my office was damaged?

No, no documents are required.

What exactly is free?

For the first two months there is no rent or operating costs. Utilities are paid as used.

How can I find out the detailed lease terms?

Submit a request and a manager will call you within an hour and explain everything about the cost and the contract terms.

How soon can I move in?

The day after the contract is signed.

Are furnished offices available?

Yes, furniture can be provided by agreement.

How much space can I lease?

From 50 to 1,000 m² per company.

Until when is the offer valid?

Applications are accepted until November 1, 2026, or until all 4,000 m² have been allocated.
